The concept

The compact you remember. Refit for the boardroom.

In 1989, in Swindon, England, a father took an old cosmetic compact and tucked a tiny dollhouse inside it for his daughter. The case was three inches wide. Hinged. Heart-clasped. Inside: a whole world. Polly's house. Polly's salon. Polly's stable. Thirty-seven different worlds in the first year alone — each one self-contained, each one ready to slip into a coat pocket and travel anywhere imagination needed to go.

We borrowed the idea. Happy Hunt.ai is the executive's compact. Hinged. Heart-clasped. Inside: a strategy room, a boardroom, a vendor salon — staffed by your private agent-run concierge, sized to slot into the exact moments you need her. Snap it open before a meeting. Snap it shut and walk in prepared. Carry the whole transformation in your pocket. That was always the magic. We just made one for grown-ups.
